SMS Terms & Conditions

Homelab Notifications · Operated by Gavin Hernandez · Effective October 10, 2026

1. Program description

Homelab Notifications is a private, non-commercial program operated by Gavin Hernandez. It sends automated text messages about the status of a personal home server, for example when a disc copy starts or finishes. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

2. Consent and opt-in

Messages are sent only to mobile numbers belonging to the program operator, which the operator adds to the server's notification configuration. Adding a number constitutes express consent to receive these automated messages. There is no public sign-up. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies with server activity, typically 0–10 messages per day.

4. Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan for details. The Program itself is free.

5. Opting out (STOP)

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent. To resubscribe, reply START.

6. Help (HELP)

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or email gavinhernandez13531@gmail.com.

7. Carrier disclaimer

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to effective transmission from your wireless carrier and network availability.

8. Privacy

Information collected through the Program is handled as described in our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
