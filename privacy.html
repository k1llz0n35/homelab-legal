Privacy Policy

Homelab Notifications · Operated by Gavin Hernandez · Effective October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gavin Hernandez ("we", "us"), operator of the Homelab Notifications text-message program (the "Program"), collects, uses and protects information in connection with the Program.

1. Information we collect

  • Mobile phone number(s) that the operator adds to the Program to receive alerts.
  • Message content: automated status text about the operator's personal home server, such as "Rip complete: Movie Name".
  • Delivery information that our messaging provider generates, such as send time and delivery status.
  • Opt-out and help requests: replies such as STOP or HELP.

We do not collect names, addresses, payment information, location data or any other personal information through the Program.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to send the automated alerts the operator has requested, to honor opt-out (STOP) and help (HELP) requests, and to troubleshoot message delivery.

3. How we share information

Phone numbers and message content are processed by our SMS service provider, Twilio Inc., only to deliver messages. Twilio handles this data under its own privacy policy. We do not sell, rent or trade your information.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent. This information will not be shared with any third parties.

We may disclose information if required by law, such as in response to a valid subpoena or court order.

4. Data retention

Phone numbers are kept only while they are configured to receive alerts, and are deleted when removed from the Program. Message logs kept by our messaging provider follow that provider's retention settings.

5. Security

Credentials and phone numbers are stored only on privately operated servers that are not publicly accessible, and are never published or committed to source control. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect this information.

6. Your choices

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. You can also contact us at the email below to have your number removed.

7. Children's privacy

The Program is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, with an updated effective date.
